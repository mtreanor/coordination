How a Run works

Agents, each working for its own principal, do a piece of work together. Alongside the work runs a separate social channel, modeled in RUE. These diagrams show how the two meet, what happens inside one RUE tick, and what an agent is shown when it has to choose.

1

The system

The work and RUE never talk to each other directly. The Run sits between them and is the only part that asks the agents anything. The numbers on the arrows are a round's phases, in order.

2

One RUE tick: three stages where agents choose

Each round, the social layer runs one tick of the tick plan full. Its three stages differ in who is asked and what the choices do. They share one machinery for building the menu, shown underneath.

3

What an agent is shown

Agents are stateless. Every decision arrives as one complete Decision Request, built from up to four sources. A work decision and a social decision share the system prompt but differ below it.

4

Who knows what

What an agent may know is decided by where RUE records a fact, not by a filter applied later. The work says who witnessed each event. Every Briefing renders the shared world, then that agent's own store.
